Propose a widget

Share a widget you made with the editor of your deck, or keep it just for you: it's your choice.

How it works

  1. Open the control panel of your deck and make a widget in Crea un widget.
  2. In I tuoi widget, press Proponi al market next to it: this page opens with your widget.
  3. Choose: send it to the market, or keep it on your deck.
