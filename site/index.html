Turn an old tablet or your phone into a deck for your PC: music, Discord, audio, screens, Claude Code, GitHub, your apps and shortcuts. Build your own widgets in a few clicks, or install them from the market.

Get Vibe Deck Browse the market

Works on Android 4+ tablets and on any phone, on the same Wi‑Fi as the PC.

Everything at your fingertips

Pages for your tools

Music, audio mixer, Discord, screens and virtual desktops, Claude Code sessions, GitHub, Ollama, games, Blender, DaVinci, Unreal.

Old tablets welcome

The tablet only draws: the PC does the work. Made for Android 4 browsers, light on battery.

Your HOME

Drag widgets on a grid from the control panel, resize them, pick a theme: Classic, Material, Neon, Neo‑brutalist.

Widgets without code

A shape, a data source, what to show, what a tap does. The editor in the control panel writes the JSON for you.

Voice commands

“Pause”, “volume up”, “lock the PC”: dictate on the tablet, the PC acts.

Safe by design

The tablet only says “button N”: what runs is decided on the PC. Market plugins are JSON only, checked and fingerprinted.

Make your own widget

  1. What to show — a big number, bars, on/off, text, buttons, voice commands, a list, a slider.
  2. Where the data comes from — your PC, an internet address without keys, a file, a command. Press Try the source and the data become buttons.
  3. What a tap does — open an app, a shortcut, a link, music, volume…
  4. Save — it's on your tablet. Keep it for yourself, or propose it to the market for everybody.

Market

Widgets and pages made by the community, checked before they go in: JSON only, no programs. Press Install to open the market in your control panel.
